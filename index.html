🍋 Lemon Garden

An endless garden with dogs, cats, crows and lemons. Pet them, feed them, make friends and enjoy spring!

On a computer

  • W A S D – move · Shift – sprint
  • Left click – pet, take food, serve the plate, sleep in the tent
  • 1–5 – select an inventory slot · E – use it (eat a lemon)
  • F – call your friends to follow you
  • Drag with the mouse – rotate · Wheel – zoom

On a phone

  • On-screen arrows – move · SPRINT button – run
  • Tap an animal, the food, the plate, the tent or Capi Food
  • Tap a selected slot again – use it (eat a lemon)
  • Call button – your friends follow you
  • Drag with your finger – rotate the camera

Unsupported browser

The game only works in Safari and Chrome. Open the link in one of them.
