      🍋 Lemon Garden

      An endless garden with Capi the dog, June the cat, crows and lemons. Feed them, make friends, take them to the vet every 3 days and to the pet parks, and light the night with torches!

      On a computer

      • W A S D – move · Shift – sprint
      • Left click – pet, shop, serve a plate, open the chest, sleep in the tent
      • 1–7 – select an item · E or click the ground – eat a lemon / place a torch
      • F – call Capi and June to follow you
      • M – map: click it to set a waypoint and walk there
      • V or 🎥 – switch between third person and first person
      • Bring 7 lemons to the abandoned Chest Shop to renovate it and unlock torches
      • Chest Shop: torches and name tags (🍋 × 5) to rename your pets
      • Pick blueberries in the forests (max 6) and serve them: the next vet check-up moves one day later
      • Lemonade stand: make lemonade, visitors buy it for 🪙 · Lemon Market: accessories, garden decorations, fishing rod
      • 📋 Tasks: daily missions for coins · seasons change every 4 days (winter: the river freezes, skate on it!)
      • Drag with the mouse – rotate · Wheel – zoom

      On a phone

      • On-screen arrows – move · SPRINT button – run
      • Tap an animal, a shop, a plate, the chest or the tent
      • With a lemon selected, tap the screen to eat it · with a torch, tap the ground to place it
      • Call button – Capi and June follow you
      • 🗺 or the minimap – tap the map to set a waypoint and walk there
      • 🎥 – switch between third person and first person
      • Bring 7 lemons to the abandoned Chest Shop to renovate it and unlock torches
      • Chest Shop: torches and name tags (🍋 × 5) to rename your pets
      • Pick blueberries in the forests (max 6) and serve them: the next vet check-up moves one day later
      • Lemonade stand: make lemonade, visitors buy it for 🪙 · Lemon Market: accessories, garden decorations, fishing rod
      • 📋 Tasks: daily missions for coins · seasons change every 4 days (winter: the river freezes, skate on it!)
      • Drag with your finger – rotate the camera
